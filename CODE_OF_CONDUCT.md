# Code of conduct

Retric projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't tolerated.

Report unacceptable behavior to **contact@retric.ai**. Reports are handled confidentially.
